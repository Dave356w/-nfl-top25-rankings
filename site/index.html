What the numbers mean

Proj FP is Sleeper's weekly projection for the player in half-PPR scoring, the same scoring Yahoo DFS uses. Sleeper folds injuries into that projection, so a player it does not expect to play projects at zero and is not ranked.

Role is the player's rank within his own alignment slot on the Sleeper depth chart, so all three receivers in a three-wide set read as starters rather than as a starter and two reserves. Depth is the flat opportunity rank within team and position, ordered by that role and then by projection, and is what the volatility model is keyed on. Quarterbacks are ordered by projection, so the one Sleeper expects to start is the starter even before its chart moves.
