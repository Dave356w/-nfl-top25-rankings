Method

  1. Team strength from the same projection snapshot as the rankings: starters' projected half-PPR points (QB1, RB1–2, WR1–3, TE1) and the team's DEF projection.
  2. Points model, fitted on 2021 onward: . The projected margin is home points minus away points.
  3. Median map, refit before each week from earlier games only (8-week half-life): bands of |margin| under 3, 3–7, 7–10 and 10+, each replaced by the weighted median final margin of the side the projection favoured.
  4. Model side: the mapped margin against the nflverse market line; a tie goes to the favourite.
  5. Freezing: each game's row freezes at the last update before kickoff and is graded from nflverse final scores.

Lines: nflverse schedules (open data). nflverse does not record when a line was set, so a line can lag the market. Projections: Sleeper, ESPN and CBS (see the rankings).
